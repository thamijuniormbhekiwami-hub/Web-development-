About Aseptic Fabric Softener

History

Aseptic Fabric Softener was recently established in 2026, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal by Ndumiso Dabha Xaba who is the sole proprietor of the company. The owner of the company uses qualifications obtained online and manufactures and distributes high quality, affordable fabric softeners and laundry and kitchen care products to households and laundromats across KwaZulu-Natal.

Mission

The company's mission is to deliver long-lasting freshness and softness to every wash while being gentle on the fabrics and the environment. To ensure every hug, baby cuddle and night's sleep is free of hidden irritants.
